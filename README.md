## About

I'm a **PHP developer specializing in WordPress and WooCommerce**. At **Tamland**, an online education platform, I build and maintain custom plugins, Elementor widgets, WooCommerce customizations, REST endpoints, and role-based dashboards. A big part of my work is performance: auditing queries, removing N+1 patterns, and making reports and stats fast.

My chemistry background (BSc at Shahid Beheshti University) shapes how I work: I debug systematically and want to understand *why* a system behaves the way it does before I trust it in production. Outside WordPress, I build side projects in React/TypeScript and Python.
